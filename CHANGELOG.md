# CarbsTagSniper

## v1.0.0

First public release for World of Warcraft: Forever.

- Creates and maintains the `CTSnipe` character macro that targets your quest mob and tags it
  with an instant class ability, ready to spam on the mouse wheel.
- Does nothing if the mob isn't up or is dead, and mutes error speech while you spam.
- `/cts target`, `/cts spell`, `/cts mark`, `/cts quiet`, `/cts update` and `/cts show`
  commands; the tag spell is saved per character.
